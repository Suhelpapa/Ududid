This Privacy Policy explains how GTrade ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

1. About GTrade

GTrade is an Aviator-style game intended for entertainment. The coins shown in the App are virtual game points only. They have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, sold, withdrawn, or redeemed for cash or other items of value. The App does not support real-money deposits or withdrawals.

2. No account or login

GTrade does not require users to create an account, register, or log in. The App does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment details to play.

3. Information collection

The App is designed to work without asking you for personal information. The developer does not use the App to request or collect account, contact, or payment information.

Game settings or progress may be stored locally on your device, if the App uses local storage. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

Important: This statement should be checked against the final APK. If the App includes analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-save, or other third-party SDKs, this section must be updated to describe the information those services collect.

4. Advertising and payments

GTrade does not display advertisements and does not provide real-money purchases, betting, deposits, or withdrawals.

5. Children's privacy

GTrade does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the App, please contact us using the contact details shown on the app's store listing so the concern can be reviewed.

6. Security and data retention

Because the App does not require an account or ask for personal details, it is not designed to maintain a personal user profile. Any information stored locally is controlled by the device and may be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, use the developer contact email shown on the GTrade app's Google Play Store listing.
